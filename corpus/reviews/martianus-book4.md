# Martianus Capella, Book IV (Dialectic): English review

Changes to `corpus/library/own/martianus.nuptiis.eng.md`, sections 327–424.
Categories: **A** mechanical, **B** style, **C** fidelity to the Latin.

Across the book, the tics “in such wise (that)” (26×) and “in this wise” (14×) became “in such a way (that)”, “as when” and “thus”. The archaic register (thou, hath) is kept, because the rest of the translation uses it.

## Flagged, not changed

- **343, 407**: *symperasma* is rendered “conterminous conclusion”. That follows Martianus’s own gloss *confinis conclusio* (407), so it was kept.
- **363**: the Latin is corrupt (‹…›, “[sunt de quibus]”). The English sentence about “those which are said to be in the soul” is a guess.
- **387**: the text breaks off after “those also ‹…›”. The English keeps the gap.
- **387**: the Latin calls the pair of statements about a dead man first “neither true nor false” and then “both false”. The English is faithful to that inconsistency.
- **391**: the edition inserts ‹non›. The English follows the reading without it, which makes better sense of the argument.
- **Latin file**: in §§346, 363, 378, 402, 421 and 422, editorial brackets such as “[quod]”, “[sunt de quibus]” and “[non]” were turned into “ :[…]” by the conversion. The colon is an artifact of the conversion. Not changed.
